how it works 

  
Toaster listens to whatever your PCMac plays and writes it out as Strudel code, live: the drums on a 16-step grid, the tempo, bass, chords, key and lead line. A new song starts a fresh page. It's a reconstruction for show, not an exact transcription; steady beats work best.

  
When the kick comes back after a break, the drop effect takes over the screen. The camera features run on this PCMac; nothing is uploaded. Your picks are remembered, except the camera, which always starts off.

  
  
  

    
look

    
Vbackground: spectrum, tunnel, spiral, pitch wheel, warp, cuts, worlds, chrome, spectrogram, scenery, rain, scope, off

    
Abackground strength; with worlds, their look: night (no light skies), film, ASCII, code, ink, risograph, cyanotype, receipt, thermal, night vision, CRT, VHS, quadtree, cells, auto; with chrome, the collage on or off; with the spectrogram, its colours: rainbow or the theme's; with the scenery, its scene: auto (a new one each song), the lake, the sea, the campfire, the snow, the city in the rain, the garden, the falls, halloween (auto picks it in October), the sharks, the festival, the koi pond (click the water to feed the koi), the basement show, then auto in the theme's colours

    
Ttheme: bluescreen, blackscreen, terminal, pink, whitescreen

    
Rorb: dots that take a new form every 8 bars, moved by the kick, clap and hats

    
Pblocks: punchcard, then dominoes, then off

    
Gliquid glass over the code, swelling on the kick

    
NID card: the song, tempo, key and a 1-bit picture; again for big, again to hide

    
camera

    
Cwebcam: tinted, big eyes, ASCII, off. Frame something with your fingers (each hand an L) for a filter window; turn a hand over or wink for the next filter, open your hands to close it. A circle of your hands (index tips together, thumb tips together) opens a portal (the first shows your skull: make it in front of your face): pull apart to grow it, spread wide and hold to fill the picture, pinch for the next look (once it fills the picture, a swipe too: hold an open hand up, then sweep it sideways, left for the next look, right for the one before), the circle again to close. Hand signs: ✌️ next theme, 👍 record, ✊ hush, 🖐 back

    
Kcamera effect: cut-out, slit-scan, datamosh, windows, glass, dots, voxels, none

    
perform

    
Spacetap the beat (four taps) when it gets the tempo wrong

    
Qhush every part; Q again or an open hand brings them back

    
Dthe drop, now: the drop effect, and the backgrounds' drop moments (the scenery's toast pops, the koi gather, the sharks charge, the fireworks go up)

    
Bdrop effect: XP takeover, blue screen, not responding, off
