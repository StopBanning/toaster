how it works 

  
Toaster listens to whatever your PCMac plays and writes it out as Strudel code, live: the drums on a 16-step grid, the tempo, bass, chords, key and lead line. A new song starts a fresh page. It's a reconstruction for show, not an exact transcription; steady beats work best.

  
When the kick comes back after a break, the backgrounds answer the drop, and the drop effect, if you've picked one with B, takes over the screen. The camera features run on this PCMac; nothing is uploaded. Your picks are remembered, except the camera, which always starts off.

  
  
  

    
look

    
Vbackground, best first: scenes (a new one each song), worlds, chrome, spectrogram, cuts, fire, off

    
Athe next one of whatever's showing: the scene (auto, a new one each song; the lake, the sea, the campfire, the snow, the city in the rain, the garden, the falls, halloween, the sharks, the festival, the koi pond (click the water to feed the koi), space, the eye; then auto in the theme's colours); the worlds' look (auto, night, film, ASCII, thermal, cells); the chrome's collage on or off; the spectrogram in rainbow or the theme's colours; with the handheld on, its screens (Toaster's own, neon, LCD, the Japan edition: Toaster's own in Japanese, on a Famicom-style handheld). The footer says which

    
Ttheme: bluescreen, blackscreen, terminal, pink, whitescreen

    
YCRT: an old TV over everything, on any theme: it comes on from a white line and goes off into a dot; scanlines, phosphor stripes, a flicker and a band rolling down it, glare on the glass, the corners rounded and dark, the code fringed red and blue and glowing. The picture swells on the kick and slips into static on the drop, and each background (V) is a channel, its number on screen

        
Pthe code look: dominoes, then the handheld game (the song played on a little two-screen handheld), then the punchcard, then none

        
NID card: the song, tempo, key and a 1-bit picture; again for big, again to hide

    
camera

    
Cwebcam: tinted, big eyes, ASCII, off. Frame something with your fingers (each hand an L) for a filter window; turn a hand over or wink for the next filter, open your hands to close it. A circle of your hands (index tips together, thumb tips together) opens a portal (the first shows your skull: make it in front of your face): pull apart to grow it, spread wide and hold to fill the picture, pinch for the next look (once it fills the picture, a swipe too: hold an open hand up, then sweep it sideways, left for the next look, right for the one before), the circle again to close. Hand signs: ✌️ next theme, 👍 record, ✊ hush, 🖐 back

    
Kcamera effect: cut-out, slit-scan, datamosh, windows, glass, dots, voxels, none

    
perform

    
Spacetap the beat (four taps) when it gets the tempo wrong

    
Qhush every part; Q again or an open hand brings them back

    
Dthe drop, now: the drop effect, and the backgrounds' drop moments (the scenery's toast pops, the koi gather, the sharks charge, the fireworks go up, the fire flares)

    
Bdrop effect: XP takeover, blue screen, not responding, off (off until you pick one)
