# Radio stations

Toaster comes with no stations: the radio (in the source menu, S) plays whatever stream address you
paste in. These few stations publish their streams' addresses on their own sites for listening in
other players. Toaster doesn't read this list; copy an address from it.
Each links to the station's page the address comes from, so if one stops working, the current one
is there. Most of them run on their listeners' donations: if you listen a lot, give them something.

- **Radio Paradise**: an eclectic mix of rock, pop, electronic, world music and jazz, picked by
  people. `https://stream.radioparadise.com/aac-128`
  ([its stream links](https://radioparadise.com/listen/stream-links), with its other channels).
  Plays in the browser too.
- **WFMU**: freeform radio from the New York area, each DJ playing what they like.
  `https://stream0.wfmu.org/freeform-128k` ([its streams](https://wfmu.org/audiostream.shtml)).
  The app only: its server doesn't let other sites play it.
- **WFMU's Give the Drummer Radio**: WFMU's second channel, soul, funk, garage, psych and more.
  `https://stream0.wfmu.org/drummer` ([its page](https://wfmu.org/drummer), in the playlist it
  links). The app only.
- **Radio Swiss Jazz**: jazz, swing, soul and blues around the clock from the Swiss public
  broadcaster, with no ads and no presenters. `https://stream.srg-ssr.ch/srgssr/rsj/mp3/128`
  ([its internet page](https://www.radioswissjazz.ch/en/reception/internet)). Plays in the browser
  too.
- **Deutschlandfunk Nova**: German public radio, much of it talk, with indie and pop in between.
  `https://st03.sslstream.dlf.de/dlf/03/128/mp3/stream.mp3?aggregator=web`
  ([how to listen](https://www.deutschlandfunknova.de/info/empfang)). Plays in the browser too.

Checked October 2026. A station only goes on this list if it publishes the address itself for other
players and nothing found in its terms objects; some popular ones (SomaFM, for one) ask apps not
to use their streams.
