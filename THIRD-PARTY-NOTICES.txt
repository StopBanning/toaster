The ideas and looks it's after (none of their code is in Toaster, but for the orb's above):
  DJ_Dave, whose way of performing live code the code view follows: named lines typed
    from a blank window, lighting up as they play. Toaster is an independent project,
    not made by or affiliated with DJ_Dave.
  Strudel (strudel.cc), the live coding language Toaster writes in.
  s1lax.art: the ID card's look.
  null.mpeg's visuals for DJ_Dave's AIRGLOW: the glass blob.
  lo.fi.sci.fi's segcam effect for DJ_Dave's videos: big eyes.
  autterp0p's hand-frame edit: the finger frame.
  libraries.dev's Thinking Orbs (Jakub Antalik and Alexandr Brinza): the orb.
  Anthropic's launch film for Claude Opus 5.5 ("There's more to discover"): the worlds.
  rentalunit5's video-synth piece "Circulation": the cuts' mirrored text and tunnel.
  3D spectrum analysers (a dial-up modem's handshake seen as terrain) and Chrome Music
    Lab's Spectrogram (Google Creative Lab): the spectrogram's landscape.
  Claude FM ("music for thinking and building"), the Claude YouTube channel's live stream
    drawn as ASCII art, its fisher in a boat on a misty lake, its campfire in the woods and
    its witch on a broomstick across a great moon among them: the scenery, its toaster
    fishing from a rowboat, its campfire scene and its Halloween scene.
  days_of_93's Days of Glitch, a visual mangler that cuts a picture into moving cells:
    the worlds' cells look.
  cure.visual's portals, made in TouchDesigner: the portals.
  ttyproj's "Transparent Distortion", made in TouchDesigner: the glass look.
  design4me__'s x-ray hover, a Framer component that turns a head to a skull under the
    cursor: the portals' skull look.
  stackedlol's ascii-sharks, sharks and fish drawn as ASCII on a canvas: the sharks
    scene's look (a tile of colour under each glyph, a shimmering hide) and the way its
    sharks swim.
